
# Responsible AI and Evidence-Based Answers

Responsible AI requires privacy protection, bias assessment, transparency, and careful use of evidence.

Customer data should be minimized. Privacy means collecting, retaining, and exposing only the customer information necessary for a legitimate purpose. The demonstration knowledge base uses general guidance rather than real personal customer information.

Analytics teams should assess bias across customer segments and relevant groups. A model or analysis may produce unfair outcomes when data is incomplete, unrepresentative, or affected by historical patterns. Findings should include relevant limitations and should not assume that every group is represented equally.

Business answers should distinguish measured facts from assumptions and recommendations.

When retrieved documents do not support an answer, the system should say that the available evidence is insufficient. It should not invent metrics, policies, or source quotations.

Citations should identify the documents supporting important claims. Retrieval relevance does not guarantee that a document entails a claim. Generated answers require separate grounding and factuality evaluation.
