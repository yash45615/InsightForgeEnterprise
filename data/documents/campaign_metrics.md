
# Campaign Metrics Guide

Impressions count how many times a campaign advertisement was displayed.
Clicks count interactions with an advertisement.
Conversions count the tracked desired actions attributed to a campaign.

Click-through rate (CTR) is clicks divided by impressions. If impressions
are zero, CTR should be reported as undefined rather than dividing by zero.

Conversion rate is conversions divided by clicks. If clicks are zero,
conversion rate is undefined.

Cost per conversion is campaign spend divided by conversions. If conversions
are zero, cost per conversion is undefined.

These measures describe campaign activity. They do not, by themselves,
prove that a campaign caused an increase in revenue.
